Overview

Worked as a UX/UI Designer Intern on an enterprise B2B SaaS platform, focusing on designing dashboard experiences that transform operational data and product requirements into clear, usable, and development-ready interfaces.

Responsibilities

  • Analyzed product requirements and user needs to understand dashboard goals, information priorities, and expected user interactions.
  • Created user flows and interface structures to organize complex workflows and data into clear and understandable experiences.
  • Designed responsive dashboard interfaces for enterprise web applications, considering usability, layout hierarchy, and development feasibility.
  • Translated operational and system data into meaningful UI components, including dashboard summaries, data visualizations, and information displays.
  • Applied information hierarchy and prioritization to ensure important data could be quickly understood by users.
  • Iterated on designs based on feedback, refining layouts, flows, and interface elements throughout the design process.
  • Considered underlying system and data structures when designing interfaces to ensure designs could be realistically implemented.
  • Participated in Discovery StandUps and team discussions to review progress, requirements, and design direction.
